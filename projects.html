WheresMyWater.org

Founder · Jul 2026 – Present · Scottsdale, AZ

Built after the Rio Verde Foothills water crisis left hundreds of Arizona families searching for answers. Enter any Arizona address and WheresMyWater.org shows your water provider, nearby water haulers, and other resources in one place.

The free tool pulls together public data from state and federal sources so people can find help before an emergency. It reached 28,840 unique visitors in the last 30 days and is now expanding to the rest of the United States.

NASA Space Apps Challenge — Team Trojans

Team Leader · Phoenix, AZ

I lead Team Trojans at NASA's Space Apps Challenge in Phoenix. Our challenge: build health-monitoring software for astronauts on long-duration missions.

Deep-space radiation, isolation, and altered gravity can cause immune changes, bone loss, cardiovascular events, and behavioral health problems, and far from Earth, astronauts have to spot those changes themselves. Our software will gather their health indicators and help them evaluate and act on their own health.

I'm building the team across data analysis, machine learning, visualization, UI/UX, space medicine, and writing.
