Strygonia

Co-Founder · Firmware & Software · 2026 – Present

Strygonia makes precision bench instruments for embedded engineers. Its first product, BlackBox, is a flight recorder for circuit boards. You clip it onto a breadboard, Raspberry Pi, or custom PCB and leave it running. It keeps a rolling record of power-rail voltage, current, UART output, resets, and GPIO on one shared clock, so when a board crashes you scrub back and watch what happened.

I write the STM32 firmware and the desktop timeline app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. I also built strygonia.com, where preorders are open for the $198.99 Founder's Edition through an embedded Stripe checkout.

Arktos Systems

Co-Founder · May 2025 – Present · Phoenix, AZ

Two-person hardware startup building BlackBox, a standalone flight-recorder PCB that captures voltage, current, GPIO, and UART activity from a target board for embedded debugging. I own all the firmware and software, built on an STM32H743 with microSD logging.

Took V1 from schematic to fabricated, assembled hardware through the JLCPCB PCBA pipeline, including the BOM, CPL files, and real LCSC part sourcing. I built the company site in React, TypeScript, and Vite on Vercel, and I also handle packaging design, ads, and shipping.
