Research with an NAU Professor

Reviving NASP

Genomics Research · Northern Arizona University · 2026
  • Teamed up with Dr. Jason Sahl, Associate Professor of Biology at NAU, to rescue NASP, the genomic analysis tool he published for comparing bacterial genomes
  • It crashed on first use and had gone six years without a new build. I tracked down 4 root causes plus a hidden build blacklist across 25 logged test runs
  • Got my fix merged into bioconda, shipping the first new NASP builds in six years. Results matched the expected output byte for byte
