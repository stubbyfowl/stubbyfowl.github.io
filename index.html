WheresMyWater.org

Founder · Nonprofit · 2026
  • Built after the Rio Verde Foothills water crisis left hundreds of Arizona families without answers
  • Enter an address and it shows your water provider, nearby water haulers, and other resources
  • Combines public state and federal data in one free tool. Now expanding nationwide

Strygonia

Co-Founder · Hardware Company · 2026
  • Makes BlackBox, a flight recorder for circuit boards. It records voltage, current, serial logs, and resets, so engineers can replay a crash instead of trying to reproduce it
  • I write all the STM32 firmware and the desktop replay app for macOS, Windows, and Linux
  • Preorders are open for the $198.99 Founder's Edition, with checkout on a site I built
